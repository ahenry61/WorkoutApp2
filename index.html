How to Build Strength

The Two Types of Lifts

Build-Up Lifts (Heavy Compounds): Bench, Squat, Deadlift, Overhead Press, Weighted Pull-ups, Barbell Row. Do 3-4 warm-up sets, gradually adding weight, then hit your working sets.

Start-Here Lifts (Accessories): Lateral raises, curls, flys, leg curls, etc. Start with your working weight, maintain across all sets.

Warmup Protocol (for Build-Up lifts)

Example: Working set = 185 lbs Bench

• Set 1: Bar (45 lbs) × 10 reps

• Set 2: 95 lbs × 8 reps

• Set 3: 135 lbs × 5 reps

• Set 4: 165 lbs × 3 reps

• Working Sets: 185 × 5-6 reps (4 sets)

When to Add Weight

If you hit the TOP of the rep range on ALL working sets with good form → add weight next session.

Compound lifts: +5 lbs (or 2.5 lbs on small lifts)

Bodyweight (Pull-ups, Dips): +5 lbs added weight

Accessories: +2.5-5 lbs or add 1-2 reps

Strength + Hypertrophy Split

Each workout has both:

• Strength work (5-6 reps): Build raw strength on main lift

• Hypertrophy (8-12 reps): Build muscle size

• Finishers (12-15 reps): Pump and definition

For Your Specific Goals

Wider shoulders: Lateral raises 4x/week, varying angles. Don't neglect rear delts.

Chest strength: Bench press progression is king. Heavy 5-6 reps + incline hypertrophy.

Back definition: Mix vertical pulls (pull-ups, pulldowns) and horizontal (rows). Train back 2x/week.

Visible abs: 90% diet, 10% direct ab work. Cable crunches and hanging leg raises 2-3x/week.

Midsection weight: Caloric deficit + strength training preserves muscle while losing fat. Don't crash diet.

Recovery Rules

• Don't train the same muscle group hard 2 days in a row

• Sleep 7-8 hours minimum

• Take a deload week every 6-8 weeks (reduce volume 40%)
