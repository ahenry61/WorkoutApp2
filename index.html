Progression Rules

When to increase weight:

If you hit the top of the rep range on all sets (e.g., 8 reps when prescribed 6-8), add weight next week.

Bench/OHP: +2.5-5 lbs

Squat/Deadlift: +5-10 lbs

Accessories: +2.5 lbs or add 1-2 reps

Program Structure:

Weeks 1-4: Build phase with foundational exercises

Weeks 5-8: Progression phase with advanced variations and heavier weights
